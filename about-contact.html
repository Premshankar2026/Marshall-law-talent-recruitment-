Who we are

Liftlife is a local initiative, not an outside company or franchise. We bring together rural and urban youth from around Bhagalpur and give them a real, structured way to earn part-time — through our Store and our Education & Tutors network.

Every team is built on the same idea: when individuals help each other progress, everyone earns more. There are no hidden layers or outside investors — just local people building something together, one team at a time.
