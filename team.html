1 Rules & Guidelines

⚠️
Commission is based on team progress, not a fixed salary. Your earnings grow as your team hits its targets together — there is no fixed monthly pay.
  • 1

    Attend weekly team calls. These are where targets and updates are shared — attendance is expected.

  • 2

    Log daily activity before 8 PM. Update the tracker every working day so your progress is counted correctly.

  • 3

    Respect your team members. No side-dealing with leads or customers outside official Liftlife channels.

  • 4

    Escalate issues within 24 hours. Flag any customer or store issue to your team lead the same day it happens.

  • 5

    Complete training in your first week. New members should finish all onboarding materials before taking on tasks.
